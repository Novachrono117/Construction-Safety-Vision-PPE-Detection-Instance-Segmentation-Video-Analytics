# Generative AI usage declaration

Generative AI tools were used in the development of Construction Safety Vision.
The repository records Claude Code-assisted development; OpenAI Codex assisted
with the Phase 12B delivery and documentation work. This was author-directed
work, not an autonomous project generated without human oversight.

Assistance covered code implementation and review, debugging, experiment and
protocol planning, documentation, and test generation and refinement. Generated
suggestions were subject to review and verification through the documented
project checks. Experimental decisions, including protocol and model-selection
decisions, were reviewed by the author, Vinicius Gomes.

The final metrics derive from executed project artifacts. Training and evaluation
ran through the documented project code; AI-generated text is not a source of
experimental measurements. Committed configurations, manifests, metrics and
provenance records are the evidence for reported results.

Human responsibilities included the project objectives, manual annotation and
duplicate-review decisions, acceptance of experimental protocols, and review of
final selections. These decisions are author-owned; the repository does not
establish that every associated sentence or code fragment was written without
AI assistance, and this declaration makes no such claim.

Private prompts and conversations are not included. Tool/model versions and
the fraction of individual lines assisted by AI are not comprehensively recorded
and are **UNKNOWN**. This declaration addresses academic requirement R20; it
does not substitute for the pending technical report or other deliverables.
